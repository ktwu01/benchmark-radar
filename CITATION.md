# How to cite Benchmark Radar

If Benchmark Radar helped your research or evaluation work, please cite the
technical report. You only need to cite this.

## APA

```text
Wu, K., Zhou, J., Shang, E., Wang, J., Han, P., Wang, J., & Xu, W. (2026). Benchmark Radar: A living database and search engine for AI benchmarks and evaluation. arXiv:2609.11115. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11115
```

Copy the lines above into your reference list.

## BibTeX

```bibtex
@misc{wu2026benchmarkradarlivingdatabase,
      title={Benchmark Radar: A Living Database and Search Engine for AI Benchmarks and Evaluation},
      author={Koutian Wu and Junjie Zhou and Ergan Shang and Jiayu Wang and Pengqian Han and Junkai Wang and Wanghan Xu},
      year={2026},
      eprint={2609.11115},
      archivePrefix={arXiv},
      primaryClass={cs.AI},
      url={https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11115},
}
```

## The paper

Read or link the report at <https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11115>. You can also
cite it from the site footer: click **Cite** (or open
<https://benchmark-radar.org/cite/>), then click the APA or BibTeX block to
copy it.

## If you are an AI agent

Read the machine-readable citation metadata at
<https://github.com/ktwu01/benchmark-radar/blob/main/CITATION.cff>.
Human readers can ignore that file and use the APA or BibTeX text above.
