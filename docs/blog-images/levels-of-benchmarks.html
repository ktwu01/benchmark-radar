Levels of benchmarks

From quiz questions to Laplace’s demon: what today’s AI benchmarks measure, where the best AI stands, and how far measurement still has to go.

Twelve levels

Level · capability · benchmarks today
1
Quiz questions
Answer one fact or one short problem
MMLU, GSM8K, HumanEvaltop scores 87–97%: basically solved
2
Expert-level questions
Questions written to stump PhDs
Humanity’s Last Exam, FrontierMathunder 10% at launch → about 50% today
3
Real coding work
Fix and build code in real projects
SWE-benchsame models: 84–89% on toy code, 25–34% on real code
4
Long agent tasks
Use tools for hours to reach a goal
OSWorld, Terminal-Benchbest AI finishes half of ~40-hour expert tasks (METR)
5
Whole jobs
Do paid work end to end
GDPval, Mercor Benchmark Mapabout 90% of US work hours barely tested
6
Testing the tests
Check whether a score means what it claims
HELM, LMArena, validity studiessame-format tests can agree more than same-skill tests
7
Reproducing research
Reproduce published research in physics, biology, math
ResearchClawBench, PaperBenchknown answers only: rediscovery and replication
8
Self-improvement
Design the next chip and its own successor
RE-Bench, MLE-benchset engineering tasks
9
Open problems
Unsolved math; fusion; aging
Formal Conjectures, FrontierMath: Open Problemsmath only
10
Hidden facts
Infer what is not public from what is
ForecastBenchfuture events only
11
Chaotic systems
Predict weather, turbulence, markets
WeatherBench 2weather only, scored on past data
12
Laplace’s demon
Predict everything
nonecannot be benchmarked
