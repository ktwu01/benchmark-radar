# Benchmark Radar replies

The customer is the email sender. Answer their question first, then make these asks:

1. Cite and credit Benchmark Radar, with a link, everywhere they use it.
2. If they publish a paper, cite our paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11115
3. Share Benchmark Radar with friends and colleagues.
4. Join our Discord to promote and sell their open-source tools: https://discord.gg/rXqS38mkN

Ask 4 is the hook. Make it specific to their tools.
